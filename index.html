WEB2APP · PLANNED

Prepare Your Website for Mobile App Delivery.

Web2App guides users through the entire website-to-mobile-app conversion process, even if they have little technical experience. Our step-by-step platform helps users find and verify their website URL, set up their app name and icon, configure navigation and permissions, review build settings, test their app on a device, and prepare it for publishing. From website to mobile app, we make the process simple, guided, and accessible to everyone. How your platform should guide users 1. Find Your Website URL — Help users locate and enter their website address, such as https://example.com. 2. Verify Your Website — Check that the website URL is valid and accessible. 3. Set Up Your App — Choose the app name, icon, and branding. 4. Configure Navigation — Set how users move through the app. 5. Review Permissions — Guide users through the permissions their app needs. 6. Configure the Build — Set the necessary app configuration options. 7. Test on a Device — Help users test the generated app. Prepare for Publishing — Guide users through the requirements for publishing their app.
